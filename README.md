# theORQL Community

theORQL is a web platform for professional financial research. It brings together market data, charts, historical studies, and AI-assisted investigation. Independent analysts, research publishers, and investment research teams can use it to investigate market changes and keep their evidence and reasoning available for review.

## Join the conversation

Share a market question, discuss a research method, or tell us what would make your workflow easier. For example, if market breadth improves, how would you check whether the move spans sectors or depends on a few groups? Bring the measure, time period, and comparisons you used so others can follow the reasoning.
